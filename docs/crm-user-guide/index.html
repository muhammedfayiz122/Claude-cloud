Start here

Find your way around

Every screen in the CRM shares the same frame. The sidebar on the left lists the workspaces you have access to, and the bar across the top holds search, the date, notifications and your account menu. Workspaces you don't have permission for are hidden, so your sidebar may be shorter than the one shown here.

Search and jump with Ctrl K

Press Ctrl K (⌘ K on a Mac) on any screen and start typing. Results are grouped into recently viewed items, records, actions you can take, things you can create, and screens you can go to. Use the arrow keys and Enter to open a result.

Administrators

First-time setup

The first time an administrator opens a new organisation, the CRM walks them through six short steps. Each step is saved as you go, and everything you choose can be changed later in Settings.

Admins

If a step reports that it didn’t finish, some of it may already be saved. Read the summary on the last step before trying again, and contact support with the reference shown in the message if it repeats.

Leads

Work your lead list

A lead is an enquiry from someone who might become a customer. The Leads workspace lists every lead you are allowed to see, newest activity first. The count above the table always comes from the server, so it reflects every matching lead, not only the rows on screen.

Create a lead

  1. On the Leads screen choose the blue + button (New Lead).
  2. Enter the person’s Name. It is the only required field.
  3. Pick the Source so reports can show which channels bring in business.
  4. Add an email and phone number. The phone field picks the country code from your organisation’s default.
  5. Type anything you learned in Note; it becomes the first note on the record.
  6. Leave Stage on New unless the lead has already been worked.
  7. Leave Assign to on Automated to let your routing rules choose the owner, or pick a person to send it straight to them. Choose Create lead.
Note

Your administrator decides which fields appear on this form and in what order. If you need a field that isn’t there, ask them to add it under Settings → Lead fields.

Leads

Inside a lead record

Open any lead to see everything about it on one page: who owns it, where it stands, what happened and what is due next.

Move a lead through the pipeline

Click the next stage in the stage path. The lead moves at once and a confirmation such as “Aisha Rahman moved to Contacted” appears. Every move is kept under the Stage history tab. Some organisations require approval for certain stages; when that applies, the move waits in the approver’s Approvals queue.

Your day

Follow-ups, My Work and the calendar

When a follow-up falls due, the CRM tells you even if you are on another screen. Deal with each one in place, or choose Later to come back to them.

My Work

My Work is your to-do list across every lead and customer. Overdue tasks are flagged in red and tasks due today in amber. Each row lets you complete the task, log a call, schedule a meeting or jump to the lead.

Customers

Accounts and contacts

An account is an organisation you do business with: a company, an authority or a partner. A contact is a person. A contact can act for one or more accounts, or be a direct customer in their own right.

Sales

Opportunities, proposals and approvals

An opportunity is a deal you are working: who it is with, what it is worth, how likely it is to close and when. Switch between a list and a board to see deals by stage.

Insight

Dashboards and reports

Every figure on a dashboard or report is counted by the server across the records you are allowed to see, so two people may see different totals for the same report.

Reference

Common questions

A screen in this guide is missing from my sidebar.
Workspaces appear only when your role allows them and your organisation has the feature. Ask your administrator.
A panel says I may not see something, rather than showing it empty.
That is deliberate. “Nothing here” and “you don’t have access” are shown differently so an empty list never hides a permission problem.
My lead went to a queue instead of to me.
When Assign to is left on Automated, routing rules decide the owner. Leads that no rule matches go to the fallback queue, where anyone with access can pick them up.
Times look wrong.
Times are shown in your organisation’s time zone, which an administrator sets in Settings → General.
An error mentions a reference code.
Copy the reference and send it to your support contact. It lets them find exactly what happened.
